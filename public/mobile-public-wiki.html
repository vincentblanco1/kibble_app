Critical Lethal

Xylitol (Birch Sugar)

Classification: Sugar Alcohol / Artificial Sweetener
Score: 98

Causes rapid, life-threatening insulin surge in dogs, triggering severe hypoglycemia, ataxia, seizures, and acute hepatic failure. Toxic at doses as low as 0.1 g/kg.

Primary Risk: Acute Liver Failure Canine Prohibited
Lethal to Felines

Propylene Glycol (E1520)

Classification: Synthetic Humectant & Solvent
Score: 92

Tolerated in minor quantities by dogs, but strictly toxic to cats. Felines lack the hepatic glucuronidation pathway, causing oxidative hemoglobin precipitation and fatal Heinz body hemolytic anemia.

Primary Risk: Heinz Body Anemia Feline Ban
Caution Flag

BHA & BHT (E320 / E321)

Classification: Chemical Fat Stabilizer & Preservative
Score: 48

Synthetic preservatives commonly used in rendered meat meals. Chronic daily bioaccumulation has been associated with renal filtration stress and endocrine disruption.

Recommended Alternative: Mixed Tocopherols Flagged
Caution Flag

Artificial Dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5)

Classification: Petroleum-Derived Colorants
Score: 42

Purely cosmetic additives for consumer packaging appeal. Pets cannot perceive food dyes. Associated with allergic pruritus, dermatitis flare-ups, and unnecessary hepatic processing.

Biological Need: 0% (Zero) Avoid
Safe & Vital Nutrient

Taurine (Amino Acid)

Classification: Essential Feline Nutrient
Score: 00

Cats cannot synthesize taurine endogenously. Deficiency directly triggers central retinal degeneration (irreversible blindness) and dilated cardiomyopathy (heart failure).

Clinical Role: Heart & Vision Support Essential
Safe & Beneficial

Wild Alaskan Salmon

Classification: Bioavailable Animal Protein
Score: 02

High biological value protein rich in EPA and DHA marine Omega-3 fatty acids. Supports renal function in felines and reduces inflammatory skin reactions in canines.

Clinical Benefit: Omega-3 EPA / DHA Optimal
