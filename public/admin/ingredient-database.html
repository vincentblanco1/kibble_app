Species-Specific Safety Reference

Registry Guidelines

Dogs and cats metabolize ingredients differently, so every species an ingredient applies to gets its own classification, its own risk index, and its own clinical details (the wording adapts to Safe, Caution, or Toxic). If an ingredient isn't assessed for one species, mark it Not rated for that species instead of guessing - this is exactly what pet owners see on the Risk Library and Label Analysis screens in the app, so what's entered here directly drives what they're shown. If OCR is matching the same real ingredient under different names, register the alternates under Also Known As when adding or editing an entry, or use the Merge button on an existing duplicate to fold it into the correct one.
