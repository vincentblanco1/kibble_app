Critical Lethal Canine Fatal

Xylitol (Birch Sugar)

Classification: Sugar Alcohol

Causes rapid, massive insulin surge in canines resulting in severe hypoglycemia, seizures, and acute hepatic necrosis. Toxic at 0.1 g/kg.

Primary Risk: Liver Failure Prohibited
Lethal to Felines Glucuronidation Defect

Propylene Glycol

Classification: Synthetic Humectant

While tolerated in minor quantities by dogs, cats lack glucuronidation pathways. Triggers Heinz body hemolytic anemia.

Primary Risk: Heinz Body Anemia Feline Ban
Caution Flag Synthetic Preservative

BHA & BHT

Classification: Chemical Fat Stabilizer

Synthetic preservatives. Bioaccumulation from long-term consumption has been associated with endocrine disruption and allergic dermatitis.

Caution Flag Cosmetic Additive

Artificial Dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5)

Added for marketing appeal; pets cannot distinguish food colors. Linked to allergic skin flare-ups and unnecessary renal filtration stress.

Wild Salmon

Classification: Marine Animal Protein

Complete essential amino acid profile. Rich in Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) that promote renal vascular perfusion and joint mobility.

Taurine

Classification: Essential Amino Sulfonic Acid

Cats cannot synthesize taurine endogenously. Deficiency directly induces dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM) and central retinal degeneration.

Benefit: Heart & Vision Vital
Critical Lethal AAFCO Prohibited

Xylitol (Birch Sugar)

Classification: Sugar Alcohol

Who Is Affected? (Species Tolerance)

🐶 Canine (Dogs) Lethal Hazard

Severe toxic response. Triggers massive insulin release and acute hepatic failure.

🐱 Feline (Cats) Low Documented Risk

Does not trigger severe insulin spike, but offers zero biological benefit.

Clinical Mechanism & Danger

In dogs, xylitol is absorbed into the bloodstream within 30 minutes, stimulating a potent release of insulin from the pancreas. This leads to profound hypoglycemia, hypokalemia, weakness, collapse, and seizures.

Common Aliases & E-Numbers:

Birch bark extract, Wood sugar, E967, D-xylitol

Recommended Safe Alternative:

Pure pumpkin puree, unsweetened applesauce, or fresh blueberries.
